What it does

The record travels with the result

Give Sumex the shape of a computation. It compiles the intent, executes it under the constraints you set, and returns a result that can defend itself.

Content-addressed

The graph and its inputs have a fingerprint. The same computation always has the same one; a changed computation never does. Tamper-evident by construction.

Honest trust tiers

A result is Computed, Reproduced, and/or Signed — independent and composable. Signed is not reproduced; reproduced is not proven. The label never overstates.

Enforced limits

You declare time, memory, and size limits. The runtime refuses to exceed them and fails clearly — oversized work is rejected before it allocates, not after it crashes.

It can find the computation

Give it inputs, a target, and constraints, and it searches for a computation that satisfies them — then proves each candidate by running it. It proposes; the engine verifies.

Who it's for

For results that cross a boundary

Sumex earns its place when a computed result is handed to someone who has to trust it without redoing the work. When you just need a fast number, an ordinary script wins.

Model validation & internal audit starting here

A valuation or risk figure produced by one team, relied on by risk, compliance, and eventually an auditor. Reproducing last quarter's number becomes automatic instead of a manual reconstruction.

Computational research starting here

Results that feed papers, collaborators, and review. A reviewer verifies the artifact instead of trusting the author — and you can reproduce the figure from months ago.

Trustworthy compute for AI agents next

A model decides what to compute and reads the result; Sumex is the hands that compute it verifiably. Not a competitor to AI — the tool it calls when the number has to be right.

Cross-party & regulated handoffs the horizon

Lab to regulator, subcontractor to liable prime, model to auditor. “Trust me” becomes “check it yourself”: a signed artifact a third party confirms independently.

Where it is, stated plainly

Sumex is an early, working v0.1 runtime. The trust foundation — identity, reproducibility, resource safety, cryptographic attestation, bounded synthesis — is built and continuously verified on a clean machine.

It is not yet a finished commercial product: no asynchronous job model for long computations, no agent interface yet. Which of those comes first depends on what early users actually need. This is an invitation to pilot, not a pitch for a shipped product.

The honesty about limits is deliberate. Sumex's premise is that a system should not claim more than it can show — so its own description doesn't either.

Pricing will be usage-based (credits), with a pilot tier for early partners. Not yet published — talk to us and we'll shape it together.
